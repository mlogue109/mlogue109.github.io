I'm an Irish observational astronomer working in Scotland, having recently graduated from the University of St Andrews with my PhD in astrophysics ([my PhD thesis here](https://doi.org/10.17630/sta/1657)). 

I'm trying to understand how massive stars (stars at least eight times more massive than our Sun) form in our Milky Way galaxy. I use radio and (sub)millimetre wavelength interferometric observations, primarily from [ALMA](https://www.almaobservatory.org/en/home/) and the [VLA](https://science.nrao.edu/facilities/vla), to characterise massive protostars and their birth environments. 

Here you can find my [recently-published first-author paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag1573), and [co-authored paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staf2168).
